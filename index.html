ABOUT ME

More than an editor.

I combine video editing, thumbnail and graphic design, social media management, digital marketing and content writing to create content with a clear purpose. I help creators, communities and businesses communicate through purposeful visuals and clear messaging.

My services cover YouTube and gaming video edits, reels and shorts, thumbnails, social creatives, video scripts, captions and promotional copy. I also support content planning, publishing, community engagement, Facebook ad creatives and Discord marketing, tailoring each piece to its audience and platform.

I work with Filmora 15, Photoshop and Canva to bring together storytelling, design and a marketing mindset, with a focus on clear communication and a consistent brand presence.
